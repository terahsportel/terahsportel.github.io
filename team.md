# Team

## Terah Sportel

### Principal Investigator

Terah Sportel is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Geography and Environmental Studies, Wilfrid Laurier University, Canada. She is a development geographer with 10+ years of experience designing and conducting community-engaged research in Tamil Nadu and Kerala. Her current research aims to contribute to equitable rural development by focusing on agrarian change, labour dynamics, and sustainable livelihoods, while mentoring graduate students.

## Jithin Raj R

### Research Associate

Jithin Raj R is an Independent Researcher in Thiruvananthapuram, Keralam, India, specialising in human geography and development studies. He has conducted research on sustainable rural livelihoods, globalisation, migration, agrarian change, and cottage industries (2007 – 2009, 2023 – present). He has extensive experience ethically designing, planning, and conducting field research, engaging with communities, and managing a research team.
