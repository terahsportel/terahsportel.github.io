# Opportunities

LLADR is recruiting master's and doctoral students. If you are interested in agrarian change, labour, and rural livelihoods in South Asia, please get in touch with a short statement of your research interests, CV, and your unofficial transcript(s).

I also supervise undergraduate honours theses at Laurier. I am best placed to supervise projects that examine livelihoods, labour, or environmental change through political economy and political ecology, whether the field site is in South Asia or Canada. Theses I have supervised examined land rights and an urban landfill dispute, and the history of a farmworkers' union. If you are considering a thesis, please email me with a short outline of your proposed topic.
